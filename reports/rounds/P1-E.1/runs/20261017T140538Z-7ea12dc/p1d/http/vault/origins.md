# Origins

We buy from three producing regions. Each lot is cupped before purchase, following [[cupping]].

## Ethiopia

Guji and Yirgacheffe lots are washed and show jasmine and bergamot. They are roasted light, see [[roasting]].

## Colombia

Huila lots are balanced, with red apple and panela sweetness. They work as filter and as [[espresso]].

## Brazil

Cerrado naturals are nutty and low in acidity. They form the base of the house blend described in [[blends]].
