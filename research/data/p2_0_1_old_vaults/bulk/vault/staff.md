# Staff

The roastery has three roles: barista, roaster and technician.

## Training

New baristas learn [[espresso]] first and [[filter]] second. Roasters train on [[roasting]] for six weeks before running batches alone.

## Shifts

Morning shifts start at seven. The roaster runs batches on Tuesday and Friday.
